Hello! I’m Dylann Martinez, a graduate student at the University of Wyoming. I come all the way from the great state of Illinois, where I earned my bachelor’s degree in geology from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Since August 2026, I’ve been in Laramie, Wyoming, pursuing a master’s in geology with a focus on economic and structural geology.
    
I am part of the MEDAL Lab (Mineral Exploration and Deposit Analysis) and am advised by Dr. Joe Biasi. My thesis work centers on structural geology, cross-section restoration, and intrusion-related deposits in Nevada.
