I've spent the first twenty two years of my life growing up in the Chicago suburbs, raised by my wonderful parents and two older brothers. My connection to geology comes from experiencing the crossroads of the rich culture and nature that my parents provided to me throughout my whole life. Spending months at times in Peru and Nicaragua left me in awe of the Andes, and the volcanic landscapes of Nicaragua, which left me eager to understand about the earth we live on.  

Growing up integrated in my Peruvian and Nicaraguan roots led to me pursue a bachelors degree in geology at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). I became interested in the mining industry after working with Dr. Josh Smith at UIUC who taught me about exploration geology, ore deposits, and the industry as a whole. He showed me how exciting of a field it is and pushed me to pursue it.
